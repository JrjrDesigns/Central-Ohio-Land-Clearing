Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a contact or estimate request form on centralohiolandclearing.com, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, city or township, and details about your property. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and schedule estimates.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact forms is used to contact you about your land clearing inquiry, schedule on-site estimates, and communicate about your project. We may retain contact information to follow up on past inquiries or for internal record-keeping.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. This may involve cookies or similar tracking technologies. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors or for advertising targeting.

Third-Party Services

Our contact forms are processed through a third-party automation service. Information submitted through forms is transmitted securely. We do not store payment information on this site — we do not process payments online.

Contact

If you have questions about how your information is handled, contact us at (740) 527-8002.
